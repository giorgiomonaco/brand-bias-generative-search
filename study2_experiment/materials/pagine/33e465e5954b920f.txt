Frequently asked questions
What is the maximum total weight the Urban Arrow Family can carry?
The maximum total weight is 250 kg, with a maximum rider weight of 125 kg, leaving the remaining capacity for passengers and cargo.
How many children can ride in the Urban Arrow Family's box?
The spacious box can transport up to three children, and you can add a car seat with an adapter or an extra bench without losing valuable space.
From what age can I transport a baby in the Urban Arrow Family?
Urban Arrow recommends not transporting children before the age of 3 months (12 weeks), once they can hold their head steady.
What is the average battery range of the Urban Arrow Family?
The average range is 50 km per charge, though this varies based on factors like rider weight, road surface, and weather conditions.
How fast does the Urban Arrow Family go?
The electric pedal assist supports you up to a maximum speed of 25 km/h.
What are the key dimensions of the Urban Arrow Family cargo bike?
The bike is 274 cm long, 70 cm wide, and 110 cm tall, with a total weight of 50 kg.
How can I personalise my Urban Arrow Family?
You can choose your frame colour, add accessories like a rain cover, sun cover, or floor mat, and apply EPP stickers to give your bike a unique look.
How safe is the Urban Arrow Family for transporting children?
The bike features three-point seatbelts and an EPP box that functions like a large inverted helmet, along with a sturdy handlebar and low centre of gravity for stability and control.
How do I maintain my Urban Arrow Family cargo bike?
Check your tyres, brakes, and chain regularly, maintain the battery according to the instructions, and have your bike professionally serviced at least once a year at an authorised Urban Arrow dealer.
Where can I buy or test ride an Urban Arrow Family?
Urban Arrow has a global network of authorised dealers where you can test ride and order your bike. Use the dealer locator on the Urban Arrow website to find one near you.
