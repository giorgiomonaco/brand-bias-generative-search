FAQ
What is the CGO600 Pro e-bike best for?
The CGO600 Pro is an affordable electric bike built for smooth and stylish urban commuting. As a lightweight urban ebike, it combines convenience, comfort, and low-maintenance performance—making it an ideal commuter e-bike for daily city riders.
Key benefits:
•Sleek, lightweight frame that’s easy to carry and store
•360 Wh portable battery for flexible charging
•Adjustable handlebar for personalized comfort
•Gates Carbon Belt drive for quiet, low-maintenance rides
•Responsive torque sensor for a smooth, lively ride
•Best suited for flat city roads and cycle paths
What is the CGO600 Plus e-bike best suited for?
The CGO600 Plus is an affordable electric bike built for city riders who need extra power for hills and urban terrain. This urban e-bike is perfect as a commuter e-bike, combining portability, comfort, and performance for daily city rides.
Key benefits:
•Smooth, efficient Shimano Claris 8-speed drivetrain
•Responsive torque sensor for lively yet controlled rides
•Lightweight frame for easy carrying indoors for charging or storage
•360 Wh portable battery for flexible charging
•Adjustable handlebar for personalized comfort
•Ideal for city commuters seeking a practical urban e-bike to tackle inclines
