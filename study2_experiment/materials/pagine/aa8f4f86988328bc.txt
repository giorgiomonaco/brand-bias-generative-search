Your Serene Hideaway
Introducing Pickalbatros Royal Grand Resort, your ultimate sanctuary for adults 16+ and couples only.
Nestled
on the pristine shores of The Om El Seid plateau, this exquisite 5-star beachfront resort offers a breathtaking escape in Sharm El Sheikh. Overlooking the magnificent Tiran Island, renowned for its world-class diving and snorkeling, this idyllic retreat is a paradise for underwater enthusiasts.
Immerse yourself in
luxury and convenience
, with Il Mercato and Neverland just moments away, and Sharm El Sheikh’s vibrant traditional bazaars a short fifteen-minute drive. With a 400-meter private beach, a refreshing outdoor pool, and awe-inspiring views, Pickalbatros Royal Grand Resort provides the perfect setting to unwind, recharge, and embrace tranquility.
