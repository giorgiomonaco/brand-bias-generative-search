What You Need to Know
What are the notes in DAVIDOFF Cool Water Eau de Toilette 40ml?
+
Cool Water EDT opens with crisp marine notes and aromatic freshness from lavender and mint. The heart adds depth and structure before the base settles into warm amber, sandalwood and oakmoss, creating a clean, lasting masculine signature that feels effortless from morning through to evening.
What are the best uses for DAVIDOFF Cool Water?
+
Best for daily wear when you want an easy, clean signature: office, casual days, post-shower freshness, and warm-weather occasions. Its aquatic profile with mint, lavender and an amber base suits a light, invigorating application.
