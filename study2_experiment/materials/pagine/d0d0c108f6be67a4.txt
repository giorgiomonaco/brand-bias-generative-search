Description
Vertex 05, the latest evolution of our most iconic paddle.
The best-selling paddle on the market returns this year, more complete, more responsive, more stable, and more intuitive, reaffirming its position as the most balanced option for any style of play .
Its new reinforced core and optimized Curv:aKtiv improve stability and energy transmission with every shot , offering a smooth, easy, and solid playing experience.
The Vertex 05 is the complete racket: capable of adapting to defense, attack and transitions , always with the same consistency and reliability that has made this series an icon of padel.
The pursuit of perfection continues to guide us, and with the Vertex 05 we take it one step further: the most complete racket we've created to date, developed in collaboration with our professional players to take the Total Play concept to the highest level.
Discover all its technologies below:
Curv:Aktiv:
CurvAktiv has been optimised in the new Vertex 05, increasing the incidence of its faces by 11º to reach a total tilt of 31.6º. This greater rotation in the lateral faces creates a deeper variable section along the profile, resulting in a unique and highly efficient twisted structure. The result is a racket with a more balanced vibration distribution, capable of performing better in two directions: greater structural solidity and increased reactivity in every shot, providing immediate comfort with a livelier and more controlled response in any phase of the game.
AIR POWER:
AIR POWER is the natural evolution of the Air React Channel system. With this new technology, we have expanded the size of the channel in the lower lateral part of the frame by 50%. This was achieved by increasing the length of the surfaces involved in the hollow structure. This makes Air Power a much more efficient zone compared to a solid racket or its predecessor. It adds even more energy return, agility, and lightness, making the Vertex 05 more reactive, stable, aerodynamic, and manoeuvrable.
AIR POWER + CurvAktiv:
The Vertex 05 combines Air Power and CurvAktiv technologies for total performance. CurvAktiv enhances the Coanda effect, channelling air towards the Air Power system, reducing air resistance and improving aerodynamics by up to 23%.
VERTEX CORE:
The classic essence, taken to the next level. The new Vertex heart, featuring a double diagonal bridge, has undergone a complete restyle to achieve a more triangular geometry that optimally distributes forces. This reduces vibrations and significantly increases the rigidity of the structure.
EASE VIBE:
Ease Vibe are next-generation dampeners designed to optimise performance. Integrated into four strategic holes and tested by the Polytechnic University of Madrid, they absorb up to 49% of vibrations. Their spring-shaped geometry allows for unique customisation of feel and balance.
CUSTOM WEIGHT:
The CustomWeight system consists of gel-finished 3g plates that allow players to modify the racket's balance. The Vertex 05 is designed to accommodate up to 4 weights at the top, allowing for up to 12g of extra weight and a balance shift of +/- 1cm.
TOP SPIN:
Increased grain on the final layer for more pronounced and precise spin. This enhances aggressive spin on slices and volleys while ensuring the technology is more durable over time.
HESACORE:
The new Hesacore features a renewed formula to protect your game. Its ergonomic honeycomb design increases the contact surface between the hand and the grip, reducing effort and the risk of injury while providing a firm, comfortable hold.
VIBRADRIVE:
Characterised by a high-elasticity elastomer insertion that splits the handle along its longitudinal axis, it is essential for absorbing vibrations, especially on off-centre hits.
XTEND-CARBON 12K:
Manufactured with X-TEND Carbon 12K, an advanced fibre of 12,000 biaxially woven carbon filaments. It provides a balanced medium hardness that combines power and control for a versatile game.
MULTIEVA:
A "sandwich" structure with three layers:
Outer Layers:
High-density EVA for maximum power in explosive shots.
Inner Layer:
Softer, lower-density EVA to increase comfort and control on slower shots.

Technologies
Hesacore
Discover Hesacore and its revolutionary design focused on enhancing protection and comfort in your game.
Thanks to its advanced elastomer, this grip filters and minimizes unwanted vibrations, preventing joint discomfort in your arm and allowing you to concentrate exclusively on your performance on the court.
Its ergonomic honeycomb structure maximizes the contact surface between your hand and the grip, allowing your fingers to rest naturally on its concave faces.
This design drastically reduces muscle strain and the risk of injury, providing a firm, secure, and comfortable grip that guarantees absolute control of the racket at all times while reducing perspiration.
Air Power
The second generation of our Air React Channel system takes the frame's aerodynamic efficiency to a new level.
The 50 % enlargement of the channel located on the lower side creates a hollow structure that is more efficient than that of a solid blade and more advanced than its predecessor.
This new geometry optimizes airflow during the swing, reduces aerodynamic drag, and improves blade speed.
The result is faster, smoother handling with added agility, lightness, and acceleration.
Vibradrive
This technology stands out for its incorporation of a highly elastic elastomer along the longitudinal axis of the handle.
This solution acts as an advanced dampening system that effectively reduces vibrations, especially on off-center hits.
Thanks to its exceptional absorption capacity and strategic placement, Vibradrive becomes an essential component for improving comfort, control, and performance in the game.
Curvaktiv
Curv:Aktiv technology introduces a structural concept to the frame through a variable cross-section along the entire length of the racket face, combined with a 31.6° rotation of its side faces.
This design creates a unique twisted structure, optimized to work more efficiently under load.
The result is a racket that redistributes vibrations more evenly, offering immediate comfort in the hand and a cleaner feel on impact.
Structurally, Curv:Aktiv works on two levels: it increases the overall rigidity and strength, while simultaneously improving reactivity upon impact, providing a livelier, more agile, and more precise response in any phase of the game.
Furthermore, this geometry not only works structurally but also aerodynamically. The frame's curvature enhances the Coandă effect, channeling airflow more efficiently towards the Air Power system. This synergy reduces drag and improves aerodynamics by up to 23%, promoting a faster and smoother swing.
The end result is a more balanced, more efficient and more complete racket, capable of offering a total game.
Carbon Tube
Consists of a racket frame construction made of 100% bidirectional interwoven carbon.
This construction provides our rackets with maximum mechanical response, guaranteeing a perfect balance between power and control.
Ease Vibe
Ease Vibe dampeners are next-generation components designed to optimize comfort and responsiveness on the court.
Strategically integrated into four holes in the racket, this system has been validated by rigorous studies from the Polytechnic University of Madrid, demonstrating its ability to absorb up to 60% of the vibrations generated with each impact.
Its innovative spring-like geometry not only maximizes dampening but also allows for unique customization of the racket's feel and balance. By modifying their placement, you have the flexibility to adjust the racket's performance to suit the demands of the match.
Whether you're looking for absolute control or prefer to unleash maximum power, Ease Vibe dampeners allow you to adapt your playing tool to ensure a much more comfortable, protected, and effective experience.
Custom Weight
The CustomWeight system consists of 3g resin-coated carbon fiber plates, designed to provide extra weight in specific key areas of each mold. This allows for adjustments to the frame's balance and weight based on each player's needs.
Top Spin
The Top Spin finish incorporates a sandblasted texture on the final layer of the racket face, designed to maximize ball interaction.
This rough surface allows for more pronounced spin. From slices to volleys, lobs, and smashes, it enhances aggressive spin, facilitating more challenging and decisive trajectories.
The result is total control of spin, allowing you to master every action with greater precision and confidence at any stage of the game.
Multieva
Sandwich-type structure, composed of three layers of EVA rubber of varying densities, carefully adapted to optimize performance in every playing situation:
Outer layers: Made with a higher density and stiffer EVA, they are designed to maximize power in the most explosive shots, offering a quick and decisive response upon impact with the ball.
Inner layer: Made of a softer, lower density EVA, it acts as a shock-absorbing core that increases tolerance and comfort on lower-speed shots. This internal softness not only improves the feel of each impact but also helps reduce vibrations, decreasing the risk of injury and providing greater control on more delicate shots.
XtendCarbon 12K
Advanced carbon fiber composed of 12,000 high-density carbon filaments woven biaxially. Its more open architecture allows for a lightweight and homogeneous laminate that optimizes elasticity and responsiveness.
This carbon strikes the ideal balance, offering an intermediate hardness that combines power and control in a versatile way, without demanding any compromises from the player.
Thanks to these properties, the racket adapts with absolute precision to all phases of the match: it provides the necessary power in attacking actions and guarantees stability and control in defensive situations. Thus, it establishes itself as the ultimate choice for those seeking a complete game on the court.
