faq
what does the green tea scent spray smell like — what are the notes?
Green Tea Scent Spray is an invigorating, energizing fragrance in the Citrus Aromatic family, inspired by green tea to awaken the senses. It opens with bright bergamot and lemon at the top; a heart of green tea accord, jasmine, and celery seeds; and a base of amber, oakmoss, and musk for a fresh, harmonizing finish. The result is crisp and green with a soft, warm dry-down. Explore the full
Green Tea collection
.
is it a perfume, an eau de toilette, or a body mist — how concentrated is it?
Green Tea is sold as a "Scent Spray" and described as an invigorating vegan perfume, a light, refreshing way to wear the Green Tea scent day to day. Elizabeth Arden doesn't list a formal concentration for it, so think of it as an everyday spray fragrance: fresh and wearable, easy to apply generously, and simple to refresh through the day whenever you want a lift.
how long does the scent last, and how can i make it last longer?
As a fresh, citrus-green fragrance, it wears light and lively. To help it last longer, apply it to clean, moisturized skin because fragrance clings better to hydrated skin, and spritz it onto warm pulse points like the wrists, neck, and inner elbows. Layering it with the matching Green Tea Honey Drops Body Cream is one of the best ways to build and extend the scent.
is the green tea scent spray vegan and/or cruelty-free?
It is a vegan perfume, meaning it's formulated without animal-derived ingredients.
how and where should i apply it?
Spritz or dab it onto skin, focusing on pulse points such as the wrists, base of the neck, and inner elbows where warmth helps the fragrance radiate. Hold the spray a short distance from skin for an even, all-over mist, and reapply through the day as desired to refresh the scent.
can i layer it with other green tea products?
Yes, layering within the Green Tea Collection is a great way to make the scent fuller and longer-lasting. Pair it with the
Green Tea Honey Drops Body Cream
, spritzing the fragrance after moisturizing, so the scent has a hydrated base to hold onto. You can also explore other scents in the
Green Tea collection.
